Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common dental workflow inquiries.

When a clinic or lab submits a case, the project funds are secured in WorkDent Escrow. The funds are NOT disbursed to the designer until the clinic inspects the CAD file, verifies prep margins and occlusal clearance, and approves the design. If revisions are required within the original scope, the designer performs them at zero added fee.
WorkDent natively handles all industry-standard dental outputs: open STL files, colored PLY / OBJ scans, DICOM cone-beam CT stacks, and complete proprietary construction assemblies including exocad (.dentalProject), 3Shape (.3oxz), and Dental Wings files.
Standard turnaround across vetted technicians is 2.4 to 4 hours. For rush emergency orders, you can select the "Urgent Milling (Sub-90 min)" tier when posting your case to prioritize your file at the top of active designer queues.
Earnings can be transferred directly to your local bank account via Stripe Connect, international SWIFT wire, PayPal, or USDC cryptocurrency with zero delay upon completion of your 72-hour escrow clearance window. VIP Pro members enjoy instant 0-hour release.
