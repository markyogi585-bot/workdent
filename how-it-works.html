🛡️ ZERO-RISK FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE

How WorkDent Escrow Protects Both Parties

We eliminate payment uncertainty for dental freelancers and protect laboratory owners against sub-standard designs or missed clinical deadlines.

1. Deposit on Order

When a clinic or lab requests a case, funds are safely secured in WorkDent Escrow. The designer knows payment is guaranteed before opening exocad.

2. Production Sign-Off

You inspect the STL margin, contacts, and occlusal plane. If changes are needed, the designer updates the file at zero additional cost.

3. Instant Payout

Upon approval, funds are immediately credited to the designer's wallet for withdrawal via Wise, Wire Transfer, or PayPal.
