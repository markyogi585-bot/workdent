What we do.

Digital dentistry has changed how restorations are designed and delivered, but finding the right CAD talent is still harder than it should be. Practices and labs juggle freelancers, agencies, and in-house bottlenecks. Skilled designers spend as much time marketing themselves as designing.

WorkDent brings both sides into one place. Dentists and labs post a case, choose a vetted designer, share files securely, and pay only when the work meets their standard. Designers set their own rates, take on the work that fits their skills, and let the platform handle payments and invoicing.

The result is a simpler workflow, faster turnaround, and professional relationships that hold up case after case.

What we stand for.

The principles behind every case, every match, and every payout on the platform.

Trust by Default

Every designer on the platform is vetted, and every case is protected by secure file transfer and clear payment terms. You always know who you are working with and where your files are.

Quality That Shows

Dental CAD work is precise, technical, and unforgiving. We built WorkDent around designers who take that seriously - so the work you get back is ready for the chair, not the redo pile.

Fair for Both Sides

Dentists and labs get reliable talent without the overhead of hiring. Designers get a steady stream of qualified work without chasing invoices. The platform handles the friction in the middle.

Built on Relationships

Great cases come from designers and practices who know how to work together. WorkDent makes it easy to find the right match and keep working with the people who deliver.

Who we are.

We are a marketplace where dentists, dental labs, and freelance digital design professionals collaborate in one convenient platform, with everything needed to start, design, and finish a case.

We were created after listening to dentists and designers alike voice the hurdles of the process.

We solve those hurdles, from finding each other and completing cases together, to eliminating the need for multiple platforms for file uploads, communication, and payments.
