📖 The WorkDent Journey

Born on the Lab Bench.
Built for Dental Independence.

We experienced firsthand the pressure of midnight milling, peak-season overflow, and the anxiety of sending CAD files to unknown freelancers. WorkDent was built to replace uncertainty with trust, escrow security, and precision clinical standards.

🔥

The Midnight Dilemma in 2023

Every dental lab manager knows the feeling: it's 11:30 PM on a Thursday, four doctors submit 18 full-contour zirconia crowns due Monday morning, and your primary CAD designer is out sick. You take to Facebook groups and WhatsApp channels, praying that an anonymous technician across the globe can execute the design without margin discrepancies or open contact points.

Too often, files returned with inverted anatomy, missing preparation margins, or payment disputes. The dental CAD industry was growing at hyper-speed, but the infrastructure connecting technicians with clinics was broken.

🛡️

Architecting Trust: Escrow & Verification

We asked a simple question: What if dental professionals had their own dedicated network? A platform engineered specifically for STL, PLY, and DICOM workflows, backed by financial escrow so technicians are guaranteed payment upon approval, and clinics only pay for clinically precise, mill-ready designs.

In late 2024, the WorkDent Escrow Protocol was born. Every designer was vetted through test case evaluations, verified software certifications (exocad Galway/Rijeka, 3Shape Dental System), and transparent patient-safe SLA metrics.

🚀

Connect. Work. Grow. In 2026 and Beyond

Today, WorkDent has evolved from a CAD job board into a global ecosystem. Over 1,480 verified master designers, dental labs across 48 countries, and thousands of restorative cases processed every month.

Whether you are an independent exocad virtuoso working remotely, a high-volume dental laboratory scaling seasonal output, or a clinic demanding same-day digital wax-ups, WorkDent is your dedicated home.
