Preventing Margin Chipping on 0.3mm Monolithic Zirconia Veneers: Feed Rate & Bur Strategy

If you are milling feather-edge anterior veneers on high-translucency zirconia (Katana STML or e.max ZirCAD Prime), do NOT use a 1.0mm finishing bur on the margins. Switch your CAM roughing pass to 1.5mm, but enforce a 0.6mm diamond bur with a 15% feed rate reduction on the cervical 1mm. Chipping rate dropped to 0%!
