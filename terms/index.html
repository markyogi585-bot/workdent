1. What ExoConnect Is

2. Patient Privacy and Protected Health Information (Important)

You may not upload, send or share any information that could identify a patient anywhere on ExoConnect.

Your obligation as a Buyer: Before uploading any case file (such as STL, PLY, OBJ or DICOM files), you must remove all names, dates of birth, chart numbers, embedded metadata and any other Protected Health Information (PHI). How Buyers pay: Full payment is collected when an order is placed. We accept major credit cards and digital wallets, processed securely by Stripe.

Held funds: Payment stays on hold until the Seller delivers and the Buyer approves the work, or until the order completes automatically.

Fees: Buyers may pay a platform service fee, which is always displayed before checkout. Sellers who choose optional promotion or visibility features pay an extra commission on the boosted orders those features apply to.
